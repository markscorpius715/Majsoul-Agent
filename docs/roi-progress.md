## Unresolved Areas and Semantics

- Existing candidate boxes: round number, remaining tiles, honba, riichi sticks, dora, own concealed hand range, central turn indicator, and each player's score and seat wind.
- No separate independent draw area and dealer area are set up: draws are judged by spacing and timing within `self_hand`, and the red seat wind is recognized within `seat_<slot>`. All 28 items now have candidate boxes; passing completeness does not mean the crop semantics have been confirmed by the user.
- `seat_layout` is a whole-match-level state: it locks after the first consecutive recognition of three valid slots at the start, and does not re-judge occupancy on round change, only updating the current wind position, dealer, and round number. The lock is cleared only when starting a new match or explicitly resyncing; if a contradictory layout appears during the match, it stops and reports an error, and cannot silently switch mappings. This state machine is currently implemented; the specific anchor visual recognition must wait until after ROI manual acceptance.
- The entire 9424 frames were sequentially decoded, extracting one lower-area image every 90 frames, 105 in total; overviews are located at `artifacts/roi/action-survey/contact_0.png` to `contact_3.png`. This is not frame-by-frame button coverage acceptance.
- Operation-area candidate boxes have been exported at frames 0, 615, 630, 645, 6735, 6750, 6765; the comparison is `artifacts/roi/action-survey/buttons.png`. Frame 0 shows nuki-North/skip, 615/630 show pon/skip, and 6735/6750/6765 show kan/tsumo/skip. 645 is a pon character animation and should be rejected as an operable button. This box will include some pixels of the right-side North tile, so the presence of text in the area cannot be used as evidence of clickability; the spatial coverage of ron, riichi, or more button combinations has not yet been confirmed.
- Candidate boxes for the three players' rivers have been exported as crop comparisons at frames 300, 1590, 5400, 8100 (`artifacts/roi/river-review/contact.png`). Frame 8100 has hand animation above it and is only used to check the geometric range. The corners of the rectangular boxes will include some pixels of adjacent players' rivers; later, area ownership and geometric grouping must be preserved, and all tiles within the box cannot be directly counted. The current evidence does not cover complete three-row or overflow layouts.
- Additional sequential decoding was done for frames 4170, 4200, 4230, 6870, 6900, 6930. Visual inspection shows that 4200's river and main tile faces are clear; 6870 is covered by character animation; 6900 and 6930 have obvious blocky distortion and blur, and cannot be used as tile-label or clear-calibration evidence. Similarity/successful decoding of adjacent frames cannot by itself prove that image quality is trustworthy.
- `self_hand` is explicitly defined as the parent scope of all visible tiles of the player, covering the concealed hand, drawn tile, and melds; `melds_bottom` is a child scope allowed to overlap heavily with it. All tiles within the parent box cannot be directly treated as the concealed hand; subsequently, melds must first be parsed and excluded from the concealed hand candidates.
- `melds_<slot>` marks the complete spatial envelope from 0 melds up to the maximum number of melds, including the stacked positions of sideways-called tiles, added kan, and concealed kan, excluding the discard pond, tile wall, or north extraction display. Sideways melds are first enclosed with axis-aligned rectangles; during recognition, rotation/perspective normalization is performed per slot, then grouping is done by centerline, group spacing, and timing. Coarse boxes may overlap, but tile instance ownership must not be duplicated.
- The drawn tile position changes with the number of hand tiles; there is no currently verified universal fixed small box; candidate ranges are allowed, and subsequent determination is made by spacing and timing, without hardcoding a position for a particular hand.
- The center seat wind and dealer indicator may share a visual area, and cross-round confirmation is still needed. Box selection does not prove that the correct semantics can be recognized.
- The score box has only been verified in the current two frames; the display range for larger values and negative numbers needs to be checked.
- Supplementary sampling shows that 4500 and 9000 are win settlements, and 7200 is a point settlement, and they cannot be used as calibration for ordinary table frames; 3600, 6300, and 8100 have game hand animations. They should not be marked as recording corruption merely because the screen changes greatly.
- The display position of the player's north extraction tiles differs across situations: early on it is on the right side, and in later sampling it is on the left side of the avatar. A north extraction small box inferred from a single screenshot cannot prove it is universal for the whole match; the conditions for layout changes and candidate regions need to be further confirmed. This variation must not be hardcoded as a video frame number branch.
- The user pointed out that insufficient memory during recording caused some frames to be distorted. Automatic corrupted-frame detection has not yet been implemented; it is not claimed that the entire recording has been filtered. Subsequent recognition must resynchronize on unknown/conflicting states, and image sharpness or successful decoding alone must not be used to prove credibility.
