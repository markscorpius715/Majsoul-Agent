- **Fault classification** (`majsoul/live/protection.py`): `SOFT_FAILURES` (preflight_abort, safe_pause, decision_expired/failed, verification_unknown, emergency_unavailable) no longer take over immediately; they are deduplicated and counted by `soft_failure_dedupe_seconds`, and only after consecutive `soft_failure_limit` times without completion does it become `repeated_soft_failures`; independent operation deadlines still cover missed switches. `recovery_class()`: operator_stop/interrupt/stop before startup and confirmed lock screen `input_desktop_blocked` are fatal (locked); lobby with no match seen is prematch (restart Agent in lobby, at most `prematch_retries`, no client input); the rest are recoverable.
- **Handback protocol**: After takeover, client protection continues as before (auto_discard enabled first), while simultaneously starting/activating an observation-type rejoin Agent (`_rejoin`, `LiveAgentLoop(join=True, input_gate=...)`, skipping menu and opening deadlines, using the whole-match seat combination from `agent-checkpoint.json` as a hint but it must appear among fresh-frame candidates). When the window `granted` is not set, click throws FocusUnavailable (ensuring no input), and the loop itself also prohibits decision/emergency/settlement clicks. Only after rejoin has N consecutive frames (`handback_stable_frames`+2×flap) and a verified opponent-turn snapshot spanning ≥`handback_stable_seconds` does it send `agent_ready_for_handback`. Supervision sets release only when the observer also shows the table waiting and there is no popup/settlement; the protection side, on the opponent's turn, verifies in the order auto_win, no_call, auto_kita, auto_discard (last) that they are off, keeps tile sorting, and after sending `handback_released` idles with no input; supervision first stops/joins the protection process, then sets granted and `monitor.rearm()`. If closing fails, it sends `handback_rejected` and re-verifies that all are enabled; if rejoin is unhealthy at handback, restart the protection side and re-enable.
- **Debounce and budget**: `RecoveryLedger`: after handback, taking over again within `flap_window_seconds` counts as a flap, delaying exponential backoff (`rejoin_delay_seconds`→`rejoin_backoff_max_seconds`) and tightening stable frames; per hand `handbacks_per_hand` (the observer counts a hand on the rising edge of the settlement overlay), per match `handbacks_per_match`, same reason `same_reason_limit`, exceeding which locks to the lobby. `recovery_enabled=false` restores the old locking behavior.
